How the shot meter works

The bar fills in about a second. A release in the green gives a perfect shot: no scatter and a faster ball. Yellow is good. Early shots dive short into the water and late shots sail over the bar. A defender close to you shrinks the green, and shots from beyond the 5m line scatter more. The keeper remembers where you shot last, so mix up your corners.
